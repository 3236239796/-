# Asset and Audio Credits

## Original assets
The player, enemies, arena architecture, UI, particles, and synthesized audio in the initial release are original procedural work created for NULL//ASCENT.

## External asset policy
Only assets with explicit redistribution-compatible licenses may be committed. Record source URL, creator, license, modifications, and local path for each asset.

## Approved sources
- Poly Haven: CC0 HDRIs, textures, and 3D models. License: https://polyhaven.com/license
- Kenney: individual asset packs are generally CC0; verify the license file for the specific pack before inclusion. https://kenney.nl/support

No external music or sound recording is included unless its license is explicitly recorded here.
